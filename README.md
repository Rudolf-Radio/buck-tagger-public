# 🦌 Buck Tagger

Buck Tagger is an app built by hunters for hunters that sorts trail cam photos, shows when and where your deer are moving, and brings all your hunting maps and state overlays into one place.

## What it does

- **Sort trail cam photos fast.** Import straight from your SD card, tag each visit with one key (Buck, Doe, Turkey, Coyote and more, plus your own tags), and your photos get filed and named automatically.
- **Reads the date and time stamped on the photo**, for many camera brands.
- **Analytics.** See which hours, days and sites your deer show up most, along with daylight and moon phase.
- **Hunting map.** Property lines, public land, hunting units, roads and imagery in one place.
- **Phone app** that goes with the computer app.
- **Works offline.** Your photos, sites and locations stay on your own computer and phone.

### About the map information

Buck Tagger brings publicly available information together in one place. Map layers come from state and federal government agencies, counties and other organizations. **Buck Tagger does not own any of this data.** It just lets you see and use it all from one central place. Map lines are not legal survey boundaries, so always check official regulations and get permission before going on private land.
