# How to install the Buck Tagger phone app (Android)

You can use the phone app on its own, or alongside Buck Tagger on your computer. With the computer app, your photos,
sites, marks and trails sync between the two.

**Download:** [BuckTagger-phone.apk](../../releases/latest/download/BuckTagger-phone.apk) (111 MB, version 1.70)

**You need:** an Android phone with Android 5 or newer and about 400 MB of free space. iPhones aren't supported.

> To see which phones and Android versions it has actually been tested on, read **[TESTED.md](TESTED.md)**.

---

## Way 1: download it on the phone

1. On your phone, open this page and tap **BuckTagger-phone.apk** to download it.
2. Open the download from the notification, or from your **Files** or **My Files** app, under **Downloads**.
3. Android will say it can't install apps from this source. Tap **Settings**, turn on **Allow from this source**,
   then go back. (Android 5 to 7: **Settings ▸ Security ▸ Unknown sources**.)
4. Tap **Install**.
5. If a **Play Protect** box warns about an unknown app, tap **More details ▸ Install anyway**. It shows this for any
   app that doesn't come from the Play Store.
6. Tap **Open**.

## Way 2: from Buck Tagger on your computer (USB cable)

If Buck Tagger is already on your computer, it can put the app on your phone for you:

1. On the phone, turn on **USB debugging**: **Settings ▸ About phone**, tap **Build number** 7 times, then
   **Settings ▸ System ▸ Developer options ▸ USB debugging**. Menu names differ a little from phone to phone.
2. Plug the phone into the computer and unlock it.
3. In Buck Tagger, click **📱 Phone ▸ Get the phone app now ▸ Put it on the plugged-in phone**. When the phone asks
   **"Allow USB debugging?"**, tick **Always allow** and tap **OK**.
4. Buck Tagger installs the phone app, or updates it if it's already there.

Don't want to turn on USB debugging? Choose **Save the phone app as a file…** instead. Get that file onto the phone by
email, a messaging app or a USB cable, then tap it and follow steps 3 to 6 of Way 1.

## Way 3: from a friend's phone

If a friend already has Buck Tagger on their phone, it can send you a copy of itself. On their phone, open Buck Tagger
and tap **Share the app** at the bottom of the home screen. Over Bluetooth it arrives as **BuckTagger-phone.zip**, because Android won't send app files over Bluetooth. Open
it in your Files app, extract it, then tap **BuckTagger-phone.apk** and follow steps 3 to 6 above.

---

## Updating

Install the new **BuckTagger-phone.apk** the same way. It installs over the old version and keeps your marks, trails
and settings.

> If Android says **"App not installed"** when updating, your current copy came from a different build. Uninstall the
> old app first. Sync it with your computer before you do, or anything that's only on the phone will be lost.

## Uninstalling

Press and hold the **Buck Tagger** icon, then tap **Uninstall** (or **App info ▸ Uninstall**). This deletes the
phone app's own marks, trails and downloaded maps, so sync with your computer first if you want to keep them.

## What the phone app asks for

Android asks before the app can use any of these. It works without the ones you say no to; only that one feature is
turned off.

- **Location:** your spot on the map and blood or deer trails
- **Nearby devices / Bluetooth:** syncing with your computer without a cable
- **All files access** (Android 11 and newer): **Pull photos** from an SD card reader plugged into the phone
